Is the shop open right now? Your website should know.

Bukas Hours adds a live open/closed badge, noon-break schedules, holiday closures and Google-ready opening-hours schema to any WordPress site. It stays accurate even on fully cached pages.

    About this project
    Why Bukas Hours exists
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Problem
    Customers check a website before travelling to a shop, clinic or municipal office. Most small-business sites list static hours that ignore the noon break, holidays and caching, so visitors arrive to a locked door.
    
      
    Who it's for
    Philippine SMEs (bakeries, pharmacies, repair shops, cafés), clinics, cooperatives, LGU offices and schools running WordPress, and the freelancers who build their sites.
    
      
    Features
      
        
    • Live Open / Closing soon / Closed badge with next-change detail
      • 
        
    • Cache-safe browser recalculation using the business timezone
      • 
        
    • Two shifts per day for noon breaks
      • 
        
    • Holiday closures and special hours, plus a PH fixed-date holiday helper
      • 
        
    • Shortcodes and a no-build dynamic block
      • 
        
    • LocalBusiness schema with special opening hours
      • 
        
    • Custom badge text in any language; translation-ready
      • 
      
    
      
    Tech stack
    PHP 7.4+ (DateTimeImmutable), WordPress Settings, Shortcode and Block APIs, vanilla JS with Intl.DateTimeFormat, plain CSS. No jQuery, no build step, no external requests.
    
      
    Client pitch
    "Your site will tell customers if you're open right now, including lunch break and holidays, and Google gets your exact hours." Offer it as a ₱1,500–₱3,000 add-on to any small-business WordPress build, or bundle setup plus a yearly holiday update as a maintenance retainer. A Pro version could add multiple branches and a chatbot-ready status API.
